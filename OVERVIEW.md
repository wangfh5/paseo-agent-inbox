Agent inbox lists the top-level agents from every host you are connected to in one place, sorted by what needs you. It adds an **Agent inbox** screen and a sidebar row whose badge counts the agents that need attention. Clicking an agent opens its normal Paseo chat; replies, permission approvals and everything else happen there.

Agents sit in four sections and move between them as their state changes. **Needs attention** holds agents with a pending permission request, an error, or a finished turn you have not read. **Working** holds running agents. **Snoozed** and **Settled** hold agents you have put aside, and Settled also holds idle agents with nothing pending. On wide screens the sections form two columns, attention above Working on the left and Snoozed above Settled on the right; on narrow screens and phones they stack in one column. Search matches title, host, project, path and model.

Subagents are hidden by default, from the list, the counts and the badge alike. An agent counts as a subagent when its own Paseo metadata names a parent, so children of an offline, archived or remote parent stay hidden too, and a detached agent shows up again as top level. Titles are never used to guess. Agents whose workspace was archived are left out, as in Paseo's own sidebar. A **Show subagents** setting lists them nested under their parents.

**Settle** means you have handled an agent's current attention: it moves to Settled until something new happens, such as a new permission request, an error, another finished turn, or a new prompt. **Snooze** hides it until a time you pick (in 1 or 3 hours, this evening, tomorrow, next week, or a custom date, time or duration) and brings it back early on a new permission request, error or finished turn. Refreshes, reconnects and renames do not bring a settled or snoozed agent back. Both can be undone.

The plugin reads agent and workspace lists through your existing host connections and never changes your agents: it does not rename, archive, stop, prompt or mark anything read. Settle and Snooze are kept in a settings document on the host where the plugin is installed, shared by every client of that host. Install it on one host that your clients connect to; each additional installation keeps its own separate Settle and Snooze state. A host that is offline or fails to load is reported and skipped, and its agents' Settle and Snooze state is kept.

Requires Paseo 0.14 or later. Forked from Agent monitor by omercnet.
